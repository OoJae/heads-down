########## RETENTION-PMF ##########
SUMMARY: The official Clock In page (solanamobile.radiant.nexus) defines Stickiness & PMF as "How well does your app resonate with the Seeker community? Does it create habits and drive daily engagement?" The Solana Mobile blog puts it as "give Seeker users a reason to come back." Submissions must include a 3-minute demo, and the page says "a video nobody can install is not a submission." Completion is judged from the video, technical depth from GitHub commits, and winners get a code review. The real audience is small and reward-conditioned. About 200K Seekers have shipped, but only about 9,000 daily actives were being rewarded in Season 2 (June 2026), roughly 5% of devices. Solana Mobile's own strategy is to turn the dApp Store into "a daily habit rather than a one-time curiosity." On Aug 20, 2026 it re-weighted Season 2 scoring to reward "natural wallet use" over bot-like activity, and Grass made the same shift in Oct 2025. So judges and the platform are actively discounting farming. The case studies agree. Products whose retention rested on emissions or airdrops collapsed within weeks to months: STEPN MAU -95%, Hamster Kombat 300M to 41M, friend.tech DAU 77K to under 100, and Saga sold about 20K phones that were flipped for BONK and left unused. The ones that lasted sit on a real recurring need (Helium Mobile phone plan, Cash App P2P, Blackbird dining check-ins) plus a cheap daily ritual built from a few repeatable mechanics. Those mechanics are: one shared daily object (Wordle), the home-screen widget (Locket at 9M DAU; Duolingo's widget gave +60% commitment), forgiving streaks and friend streaks (freeze cut at-risk churn 21%; friend streaks +22% completion), a social feed generated from real actions (Fomo at 50-60K DAU), and scheduled events (Pokémon GO Community Day, Kalshi and Polymarket all-time-high DAU during the World Cup). BeReal (15M to under 6M DAU) and friend.tech show that a single viral mechanic without ongoing depth or shipping dies fast. ORE's grid (60-second rounds, a 1/625 Motherlode jackpot, and a 10% refining fee paid to holders who have not claimed) is a proven variable-reward and investment hook. The ORE match needs a live, user-facing integration where ORE is the main product, paid against milestones that include usage metrics. The obvious reading of "Clock In" (daily check-in, streak or pixel apps, stake-on-steps, nightly lotteries, daily-seed games, location drops, loyalty passports, fantasy trading) is already crowded with competing repos. The official meaning is simply "Clock in. Build for a month. Clock out with an app on Seeker."

IMPLICATIONS_FOR_WINNING:
- The core value must survive with the token rewards removed. Every durable case (Duolingo, Wordle, Locket, Cash App, Helium Mobile, Blackbird) meets a real recurring need. Every emission-driven case (STEPN, Hamster, friend.tech, Saga/BONK) collapsed within weeks to months. In the pitch, say explicitly why a Seeker owner opens the app on a day when there is no airdrop.
- Design a daily session of 10-30 seconds with one clear action: widget or notification, tap, Seed Vault signature, done. Add a weekly or event layer for depth (Pokémon Community Day, Locket Rollcall, Kalshi's World Cup) so the app doesn't turn into BeReal's '30 seconds then forgotten'.
- Make the daily object shared and scarce, like Wordle: one object per day that everyone, or everyone in your crew, engages with, plus a spoiler-free share artifact. It creates conversation in Seeker Telegram and X without paid acquisition.
- Use Android-native surfaces the judges explicitly score ('usage of mobile features'): a home-screen widget (Locket's product; Duolingo's widget gave +60% commitment), rich or ongoing notifications, possibly lock-screen, haptics, camera, sensors or NFC, plus MWA signing with Seed Vault on camera.
- Build forgiving streaks and social streaks: auto-applied freezes with bounded forgiveness (freeze cut at-risk churn 21%) and friend or crew streaks (+22% completion). Streaks should protect a habit, not gate a payout.
- Gate the per-person scarce parts with the Seeker Genesis Token (one device, one entry). That proves the users are real Seekers and not sybils, speaks directly to 'resonates with the Seeker community,' and puts security thinking in front of the Ethelsec judges.
- Make on-chain actions meaningful and non-spammy. Solana Mobile (Aug 20, 2026) and Grass (Oct 2025) both now penalize synthetic activity. One real value-moving transaction a day (save, mine, pay, stake into a pool) beats a daily memo 'check-in' transaction, which is what the weakest competitors are shipping.
- If going for the ORE match (it can double 1st place to $60K), ORE must be the primary, live, user-facing product, and the team must commit to post-hackathon milestones with usage metrics. ORE's 60-second rounds, Motherlode (1/625) and refining fee for holders already supply the variable reward and investment hooks. What's missing is a mobile-native daily ritual and social layer around them, framed as mining or saving rather than gambling, given optics with Toly, Mert and the security judges.
- Show retention evidence in the demo, since judges can't measure it themselves. Recruit 20-100 real Seeker users (SGT-verified) during the build month and show D1/D7 retention, streak counts and distinct-wallet transaction counts with explorer links. On-chain verifiability is what an RPC/indexer CEO like Mert will respect.
- Commit steadily all month. 'Technical depth based on GitHub commits' means commit history is scored, and winners face code verification, so Anchor programs need clean signer and PDA checks.
- Demo arc for 3 minutes: (1) 0:00-0:20 cold open on a real daily moment on a physical Seeker, with the widget glanceable; (2) the problem and why Seeker/Solana; (3) a live, uncut on-device loop: notification, one-tap action, Seed Vault signature, transaction confirmed on an explorer; (4) a jump to 'day 7' state showing streak, friends and social proof; (5) real user traction numbers; (6) vision, the dApp Store launch plan and the ORE/SKR tie-in. Narrative clarity beats production polish, and it must run on a real device, not an emulator.
- Stand apart from the saturated literal 'clock-in' interpretations. Innovation is 25%, and judges will see dozens of streak, check-in, stake-on-steps, lottery, daily-seed and location-drop apps.

IDEA_SEEDS:
- ORE Crews (Duolingo Friend Streaks × ORE): 2-5 SGT-verified Seekers form a crew. Each member's single daily tap (their pick or vote) sets which ORE squares the crew's pooled SOL deploys to in a daily crew round. Streaks raise each member's share, and a home-screen widget shows the crew streak, today's pick and the live Motherlode. ORE is the core product, and the social stake drives retention rather than yield.
- Daily ORE savings streak ('Acorns meets Duolingo, denominated in ORE'): each day's clock-in moves a user-set amount (e.g. $1) into ORE, either by buying it or by mining one small round. Refining-fee sharing rewards holding and never claiming, streak freezes cost a little, and friends see each other's jars. It rides Hardhat Chad's store-of-value framing and makes the daily transaction meaningful.
- Wordle-for-Seekers, with one synchronized daily puzzle or prediction for the whole Seeker community (SGT-gated). The outcome decides a community ORE deployment, and a spoiler-free emoji or grid share card is built for X and Telegram. It differs from sea-invaders or daily-draw because the day's collective answer drives a real on-chain event.
- Locket for wallets: a home-screen widget showing your close friends' latest verified on-chain moments (a mined Motherlode, a savings milestone, a payment note), Fomo-style with auto-generated posts and no posting burden. One-tap tips or reactions settle as tiny SOL/SKR payments, so the P2P graph grows like Cash App's.
- Seeker Community Day: a weekly synchronous event (e.g. Friday 20:00 UTC) where all Seekers co-mine a special ORE 'boss board' or run a cooperative challenge, with the daily loop building toward it (Pokémon GO calendar × ORE heartbeat). The daily hook is preparation; the weekly spike gives the demo a big moment.
- A Helius-powered 'daily wallet hygiene' ritual: one actionable card a day (reclaim rent, compound staked SKR, claim refined ORE, flag a risky approval), finished in one Seed Vault signature, with a streak and widget. It solves a real, recurring problem and would resonate with Mert and the security judges. Note that SKR staking itself doesn't qualify for the SKR prize, so use SKR another way.
- Merchant-funded tap-in rewards (Blackbird for crypto-native IRL venues such as meetups, coworking and cafés) using NFC or QR plus an SGT one-per-person check. Risk: Stampd and Legwork overlap, so differentiate by paying out in ORE and letting venues fund an ORE 'mine' pool.

THINGS_TO_AVOID:
- Literal daily check-in, streak or pixel apps whose only on-chain action is a memo transaction (e.g. clockin-canvas, cookie-canvas-mobile). They are thin, crowded and may look like the synthetic activity Solana Mobile now penalizes.
- Stake-on-steps or move-to-earn (Moonwalk is already Seeker's featured fitness app; bozPledge is in this hackathon; STEPN's -95% MAU is the cautionary tale).
- Tap-to-earn, points-for-airdrop or 'farm this app' framing (Hamster Kombat 300M to 41M; Notcoin -74%; 88% of 2024 airdrops declined). Judges will see through it, and the Seeker platform itself penalizes gaming.
- Nightly or daily lotteries and raffles presented as the product (elaris daily-draw exists). ORE-based mechanics should be framed as mining or saving with real utility, not casino confetti (Robinhood removed its confetti after backlash).
- Memecoin trading terminals, launchpads, copy-trading and fantasy-crypto tournaments (Moonshot, pump.fun, Fomo, Phantom and Jupiter own this; PumpFantasy and SwapKings are in the field). They are event-driven, not habitual, and have no x-factor.
- Social tokens, keys or bonding-curve friendships (friend.tech: 77K to under 100 DAU).
- Location NFT drops and treasure hunts (TokenRun was featured in Seeker Summer; h3re, last-home, Legwork and chainwitness are in the field) unless there is a genuinely novel twist.
- Loyalty passports without merchant-side supply (Stampd exists; Blackbird needed $85M and 600 restaurants).
- SKR staking integrations for the SKR prize (explicitly ineligible). Generic AI-agent super-apps (SeekerClaw already won MONOLITH).
- A single viral mechanic with no depth and no visible shipping cadence (BeReal and friend.tech both died of stagnation).
- PWA or web wrappers, emulator-only demos, devnet-only 'fake' flows, and one giant final commit. The official rules say 'a video nobody can install is not a submission' and technical depth is judged from commits.
- Over-claiming traction you can't back with on-chain evidence. Judges include an indexer CEO and security researchers who can check.
