########## PAST-WINNERS ##########
SUMMARY: There have been two earlier Solana Mobile hackathons, both run by RadiantsDAO, and neither was in the Saga era. Solana Mobile's own blog calls the Jul 1–Aug 4, 2025 event the "first-ever Solana Mobile Hackathon" ($100k, 10 flat $10k winners, plus a BONK bonus). MONOLITH was the second (Feb 2–Mar 9, 2026; 888 devs; 393–400+ submissions from 66 countries; winners named Apr 29–30, 2026). Clock In is the third. The most useful fact for Clock In: MONOLITH used the same 25/25/25/25 rubric (Stickiness & PMF, UX, Innovation/X-factor, Presentation), and four of its six judges (Toly, Mert, Chase, Akshay) are judging again. So the MONOLITH winners are the best guide to what these judges reward.

The 10 MONOLITH winners, all paid the same $10k:
- NOMI: a 3D pet NFT you care for daily (a "Tolygotchi").
- Echo Protocol: token drops at GPS spots, claimed with a Seed Vault signature.
- Pumpville: a social multiplayer game (MMO).
- Tramplin: staking where rewards go to a random staker every 10 minutes.
- PredictTok: a TikTok-style feed over Jupiter prediction markets.
- Cue: culture prediction markets with gas paid for the user.
- Backyard Fi and Cashflow: stablecoin yield apps.
- Foresee: social prediction markets.
- SeekerClaw: an AI agent that runs on the phone around the clock.

Seek won the SKR prize with photo-verified scavenger hunts. The honorable mentions were Cherry, Timebent, Kickr, Kinlog and Hunters of Faenora. What these winners share:
- a daily habit loop;
- use of Seeker hardware (Seed Vault signing, GPS, camera and on-device ML, Android background services);
- a familiar Web2 format moved onto Solana (TikTok, Tamagotchi, Pokemon Go, Telegram bot);
- real users or traction numbers;
- solo builders with a memorable founder story.

Several categories are now crowded by past winners plus Seeker Season apps: prediction markets (4 of the 15 MONOLITH awards, plus Trepa, dePlay, Gib Meme, Memeshot and Kickr), yield aggregators (Seed Vault Wallet now has a built-in USDC Earn Vault), GPS treasure hunts, phone AI agents, virtual pets, stablecoin shopping, gamified trading, and SKR used as an in-app currency or reward.

Clock In differs from past rounds in six ways:
- ranked prizes for the first time ($30k for 1st versus flat $10k before);
- two security researchers from EthelSec on the panel;
- a new ORE matched prize worth up to +$30k if the winner is 1st;
- SKR staking integrations do not qualify for the SKR prize;
- a 30-day window, and projects must have started within 3 months;
- AI-written code is explicitly allowed, while the 2025 round disqualified it.

Many past winners are still alive and growing (SP3ND, Nomadz, Mattle, Cherry, Trepa, CFL, SeekerClaw, Kinlog, Pumpville). Others died or pivoted within months: Scrolly shut down despite 25k users, Cashflow rebranded as Kinbay, Cue became ratio.wtf, and DojoDuel's X account is gone. Given the new Stickiness weighting, that suggests a real retention loop matters more than a flashy one-off. Finally, the organisers' Align platform shows a different scoring template (AI 20, SKR Integration 20, UX 15, UI 15, Innovation 15, Ecosystem Impact 15) and has a feature labelled "ai-screen". That hints at possible AI pre-screening, but it is unconfirmed and may just be a leftover template.

IMPLICATIONS_FOR_WINNING:
- Treat the MONOLITH winners as the calibration set. Same rubric, four of six judges returning. But Clock In names a single 1st place, so you need to be the clear best across all four 25% criteria, not just one of ten good apps.
- Stickiness (25%) is judged partly on evidence. Get the app onto real Seekers during the 30 days (Discord, Seeker community, Radiants office hours) and show retention numbers in the demo: daily actives, streak lengths, repeat sessions. MONOLITH winners cited concrete numbers (207 Echos in 34 cities, 4,000+ players, 7,000 Seeker Club members).
- Design around one clear, short daily loop, since the event is literally named 'Clock In'. Many competitors are building 'daily check-in' mechanics, so the loop needs a genuinely new core, not just a streak counter.
- Make the phone essential. Seed Vault signing, camera or GPS with on-device ML, Android services, notifications, widgets and haptics are what separated winners from web ports. Show a feature a web app cannot do in the first 20 seconds of the demo.
- Two security researchers from EthelSec are judging. Keep the program minimal and correct (owner, signer and PDA checks, checked math), avoid hot keys held by the app or its servers, and state your security design in the README. The ORE stake exploit from a missing account check is the kind of bug they will look for.
- Prize stacking: 1st place plus the ORE matched prize could total $60k, and the SKR prize ($10k) is separate. But the ORE prize requires ORE to be the primary featured product, live, and milestone-paid. Only pursue it if ORE is truly core; a bolted-on integration will not qualify.
- For the SKR prize, avoid anything already in Solana Mobile's July 2026 SKR megathread (spending, in-game currency, reward campaigns, raffles, tracking). Seek won MONOLITH's SKR prize by making SKR the stake in a hardware-verified real-world skill contest, not a reward token.
- Commit history is explicitly judged. Keep many granular, meaningful commits through the whole 30 days (SeekerClaw had 365 in 5 weeks). The repo must be clonable and runnable.
- Presentation (25%): a tight 3-minute demo with a meme-aware hook (the 'Tolygotchi' framing, a 'Wizard Toly' cameo), a founder story, and a single 'wow' moment on real hardware. Radiants: 'Do not forsake the pretty things.'
- Ship to mainnet and ideally submit to the dApp Store before judging, as NOMI did. Winners must publish within 30 days anyway, and being live helps both the Stickiness and Completion scores.
- Hedge against the possible AI pre-screen and the alternate Align rubric (AI 20, SKR 20, UX 15, UI 15, Innovation 15, Ecosystem Impact 15). A tasteful AI component, a meaningful SKR use and clear ecosystem impact cost little and cover both rubrics.
- Long-term viability matters to these judges (Toly's 'first 10k users' framing). Past winners that died (Scrolly) or pivoted (Cashflow, Cue) show that a hackathon novelty without a retention engine is not what the ecosystem wants now.

IDEA_SEEDS:
- A 'miner's cooperative' built natively for Seeker on ORE's minute-by-minute rounds: friend squads pool SOL into ORE board squares together, with live haptics and push alerts on a motherlode, a home-screen widget showing the squad's round, and squad streaks. ORE is the core product, aimed at the matched prize, with a built-in 'clock in every round' loop.
- Hardware-verified habit contracts: stake SKR on a daily commitment that the phone itself can verify (camera or on-device ML, step counter, NFC tap at the gym, a location window), signed through Seed Vault. Missed days pay into the pool of those who completed. It generalises Kinlog and Seek into a StickK-style product with SKR as the stake rather than a reward, so it avoids the megathread duplicates.
- A home-screen widget or lock-screen-first game or ritual. No past winner used Android widgets or glanceable surfaces; a one-tap daily on-chain action from the widget (signed via MWA or Seed Vault) could be a clear innovation win that also fits 'Clock In'.
- An in-person multiplayer party game using NFC or Bluetooth proximity between Seekers, played for small on-chain stakes, with only Seeker Genesis Token holders able to join (sybil-resistant). IRL social play has only non-winning precedents (ItsU, NextVibe), so avoid a pure 'proof of meet' collectible.
- A recurring 'allowance' or subscription primitive surfaced as a consumer app: parent/child or roommate pocket money, or a group savings circle with capped, revocable Seed Vault-granted pulls and push-notification check-ins. Nuntius and Kin are exploring nearby ground, so differentiate hard on UX and the social loop.
- A sealed daily prediction game as an ORE mechanic rather than yet another prediction market: every user's daily forecast is a 'hash' submitted to an ORE-style round and scored on accuracy (Trepa-style precision). Only viable if it clearly differs from the four prediction-market winners.
- An on-device AI companion that acts on the phone's sensors, not on trading (e.g. an accessibility or routine assistant like EasyA's Rhythm, using x402 micropayments for paid APIs), avoiding SeekerClaw's general-agent niche.

THINGS_TO_AVOID:
- Prediction-market front ends: TikTok feeds, social or friend markets, culture markets, sports signals, estimation markets. PredictTok, Cue, Foresee, Kickr, Trepa, dePlay, Gib Meme, Memeshot, Parabola, plus Triad, Predikt, Hivy and ManicTrade already live on Seeker.
- Stablecoin yield aggregators and vault builders (Backyard Fi, Cashflow/Kinbay, Plutus, Lince, iVaults). Seed Vault Wallet now ships a native USDC Earn Vault and portfolio tracking.
- GPS treasure-hunt, drop or 'Pokemon Go for tokens' apps (Echo Protocol, Seek, LootGo, TokenRun Treasure Trail, Baxus, Geodrop).
- General on-phone AI agents or trading bots (SeekerClaw is the incumbent winner with 317 stars and v2.1 autonomous trading; plus solclaw, xona, razeai, openseeker).
- Virtual pets or Tamagotchis (NOMI in 2026, Maneko Pet at Renaissance 2024).
- Stablecoin or SKR commerce checkouts: Amazon, eBay, gift cards, travel (SP3ND, Blormmy, Nomadz, Deks, Dinario, Zabana).
- Gamified memecoin or perps trading games and fantasy crypto leagues (Memeshot, CFL, BananaZone, Rekt, Clash of Perps, Mattle, PumpFantasy).
- Wallet-based social messengers (Cherry is dominant, with 5,000+ reviews).
- Squat or fitness proof-of-movement apps (Kinlog, Moonwalk).
- SKR used only as an in-app currency, reward pool, raffle or spin-to-win, or as staking (staking is explicitly excluded from the SKR prize).
- Photo-authenticity 'hash at capture' apps: Candor and SeekerLens did not win at MONOLITH, and Seek already took the camera-verification angle.
- Web ports or PWA wrappers, emulator-only demos, and any use of VC-funded teams or projects started before about June 8, 2026 without significant new mobile work.
- Relying on Switchboard for randomness (it is shutting down, support ended Sep 25, 2026). Use VRF alternatives such as Blueshift's ecVRF or MagicBlock.
- Cloning a past winner's format even with better polish. Judges who saw 400+ MONOLITH entries will spot near-duplicates immediately.
