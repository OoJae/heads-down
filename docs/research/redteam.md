## RED TEAM: pulse | verdict=wounded | P(1st)=0.03 | P(top10)=0.22
SUMMARY: Pulse has no fatal technical blockers. The secp256r1 precompile has been live on mainnet since June 2025 (it requires low-S, raw r||s and compressed keys). SGT memberNumber exists, so the bitmap works. Kora is production-ready, and the widget-to-BiometricPrompt flow works on Android. But three things wound it as a 1st-place bet. First, novelty: TBD ('Predict the crowd', a $3M-funded Solana project with verified polls plus markets) is already in the Seeker dApp Store and was promoted by Solana Mobile with up to $100 per Seeker. U$ER (SagaDAO) already runs SGT-gated, builder-funded polls with proof of use, and HOTSHOT, b⁴ and Cue occupy the synchronized-vote and read-the-crowd space. The name 'PULSE' is also taken by an existing daily-check-in app. Second, overclaims and design holes: the 'first public on-chain SGT verifier' claim is false (ORE's claim_seeker, Sep 2025). Commit-reveal cannot work with a bitmap alone, device reveals via WorkManager are unreliable, the timezone design is inconsistent, and the hero demo beat (a script moving an SGT) is impossible because SGTs move only inside the Seed Vault Wallet. Third, judge fit: public votes tied to SGTs clash with Mert's privacy stance and give Ethelsec a doxxing surface. AI is decorative for the 20% Align criterion. SKR paid to respondents reads as earn-SKR farming. Daily votes don't count toward Seeker activity scores. The product lives or dies on a solo builder's daily editorial pipeline. The strongest version is 'Roll Call': anonymous ZK one-Seeker-one-vote, Bayesian Truth Serum honesty scoring, and demand-funded 'Ask the Seekers' queries including from AI agents. It is a credible top-10 contender (~22%) but a long shot for 1st (~3%).

FEASIBILITY:
- [low] The secp256r1 precompile is live on mainnet, so Keystore P-256 keys can authorize votes on-chain -> TRUE. SIMD-0075 was activated on mainnet in June 2025. Constraints: the precompile needs raw 64-byte r||s (not DER), a 33-byte compressed public key and low-S (s <= n/2; high-S fails immediately). Android Keystore emits DER with random S, so the client must convert and normalize. This is doable, and the candidate already mentions low-S. (https://github.com/solana-foundation/solana-improvement-documents/blob/main/proposals/0075-precompile-for-secp256r1-sigverify.md)
- [low] One-vote-per-device bitmap indexed by the SGT's TokenGroupMember memberNumber -> TRUE. SGT mints carry tokenGroupMember {group GT22s89…, memberNumber} (e.g. memberNumber 20 on mint 5mXbkq…). The group size is ~121k and the max is 1M, so a bitmap is about 15KB today, and anyone can recount it. Write-lock contention on one account per question is fine at realistic Seeker volumes (a few thousand votes against a 12M-CU-per-account-per-block limit). (mainnet getAccountInfo 5mXbkqKz883aufhAsx3p5Z1NcvD2ppZbdTTznM6oUKLj (jsonParsed extensions))
- [medium] Commit-reveal with 'no per-vote accounts needed' -> FALSE AS WRITTEN. A bitmap records only that an SGT voted. Commit-reveal also needs each voter's 32-byte commitment stored somewhere to check the reveal, and 'guess the split' scoring needs each guess kept until finalize. That requires either per-vote PDAs (closed after reveal to recover rent) or a concurrent Merkle tree via account compression, with proofs at reveal. The 'cents per day' cost claim has to be recomputed. This is fixable, not a blocker. (Design analysis of candidate's SOLANA section (bitmap-only storage cannot verify reveals))
- [medium] Automatic reveal from the device at close via WorkManager, with results revealed to everyone 'at the same moment' -> FRAGILE. WorkManager does not run at exact times, Doze and phones that are off or offline will miss reveals, and revealed votes arrive one by one in public, so late revealers can see partial tallies and choose whether to reveal. The design also mixes '9 am local' opening with a global reveal: per-timezone closes leak results to later timezones, and a single UTC close puts the reveal at 4-5 am for part of the audience. Fixes: a single global UTC window; time-lock encryption (drand tlock) or relayer-escrowed encrypted reveals with a permissionless crank; and a streak penalty for not revealing. (Android WorkManager/Doze semantics; candidate DAILY LOOP vs SOLANA sections)
- [medium] Demo beat: 'a laptop script moves the same SGT to another wallet and tries to vote again' -> NOT DOABLE AS SCRIPTED. Per Solana Mobile's docs, the SGT 'can only be transferred between a user's wallet accounts on a permissioned basis within the Seed Vault Wallet'. Freeze, permanent-delegate and close authority all belong to GT2zuH…, so a laptop script cannot move it. The beat has to be rebuilt: a Seed Vault account switch followed by a rejected re-vote, a wallet without an SGT being rejected, and a fake Token-2022 mint with a spoofed group being rejected. (https://docs.solanamobile.com/solana-mobile-stack/seeker-genesis-token.md; on-chain mint extensions)
- [medium] The Key Attestation plus Keystore binding makes votes come from the physical device -> ONLY PARTLY. Attestation is verified off-chain, at registration only, and the program cannot check an X.509 attestation chain. A holder can bind a software key, or a key the attestation server accepted, and script daily votes. The sybil bound is therefore one vote per SGT, not proof that a human pressed the phone. Whether Seeker supports StrongBox or hardware attestation is unconfirmed. This is acceptable if the pitch says it plainly; Ethelsec will probe it. (Candidate RISKS; seeker-sms-stack notes that TEEPIN has no public attestation API)
- [low] Gasless relaying via Kora -> TRUE. solana-foundation/kora is a production fee relayer. Relayer spam is capped by SGT gating, and fees for about 10k votes a day are trivial. The relayer is a centralized liveness dependency; publish a fallback path where users pay their own fees. (https://github.com/solana-foundation/kora)
- [low] Interactive Glance widget and notification actions leading to BiometricPrompt, about 5 seconds, no wallet -> FEASIBLE. Buttons must start a translucent Activity via PendingIntent.getActivity, because Android 12+ blocks notification trampolines, and then use BiometricPrompt with a CryptoObject on a key created with setUserAuthenticationParameters(0, BIOMETRIC_STRONG). Some Seeker owners report fingerprint-sensor complaints, so a PIN fallback is needed. (Android platform rules; dapp-store-gaps hardware complaints)
- [medium] Trustless receipts for Verdicts by reading app user-state PDAs (ORE Miner, Kamino obligation) -> NARROW. This only works for protocols with per-user PDAs, which excludes most of the ~1,400 store apps: games, TWAs and swap front-ends. Everything else falls back to attestations from a Helius indexer, which is a trusted party. It also has to link the SGT wallet to whichever wallet actually used the app (often Phantom rather than Seed Vault), which needs extra signatures. Publisher claiming depends on parsing dApp Store NFTs and has a cold start. (Candidate SOLANA (6); store composition from the SeekerTracker catalog (527 games, 111 TWAs))
- [medium] Daily votes contribute to the Seeker ecosystem's activity -> NO, and this cuts both ways. Votes are signed by a Keystore key and paid for by a relayer, so the user's Seed Vault primary wallet sends no transaction and Seeker Activity Tracking gives no credit. That looks good against bot-penalty concerns, but it removes the strongest external motivator for this farming-conditioned audience, and Solana Mobile's own metrics won't see the usage. (dapp-store-gaps: Season 2 scores activity on the primary wallet)

STRONGEST VERSION: Name it 'Roll Call: the verified-Seeker census'. The loop stays widget-first and takes 5 seconds: tap an answer on the home-screen widget or notification, fingerprint, then slide to guess the split. The hook becomes the ANONYMOUS, recountable one-Seeker-one-voice primitive, which TBD and U$ER lack. (1) Identity: a one-time Seed Vault transaction verifies the SGT in-program (credit ORE's claim_seeker) and registers a Semaphore-style identity commitment in an on-chain tree. The identity secret stays in the Android Keystore and is unlocked by fingerprint. Daily votes are Groth16 proofs with per-question nullifiers, verified by groth16-solana through the alt_bn128/Poseidon syscalls and relayed gas-free by Kora. The tally anyone can recount is public; who voted what is never visible, which answers Mert and Ethelsec and defuses the doxxing concern. Keep the P-256/secp256r1 path as a fallback if the ZK proving time on the Dimensity 7300 slips. (2) Anti-herding: commit-reveal with tlock or escrowed encrypted reveals and a permissionless crank, in one global UTC window, finishing in a synchronized reveal with a haptic drumroll and a spoiler-free share grid. (3) Honesty scoring: the answer plus the split guess is exactly the Bayesian Truth Serum input, so answers that are more common than people predicted earn 'Oracle rank' and sponsor payouts. That makes farming for SKR produce honest data instead of noise, which is new and easy to defend. (4) Demand side, as the SKR and AI story: 'Ask the Seekers'. dApps, DAOs, Solana Mobile itself and AI agents pay SKR (optionally over Payment Channels or x402, taken from MELT) to put a question to the verified panel. An on-device or server AI editor rejects leading questions, respondents are paid by BTS score, part of the SKR is burned, and community proposals need an SKR bond with SGT upvotes (the whitepaper's unbuilt 'Ecosystem Curators'). (5) Cohorts instead of Verdicts: receipt-gated questions ('among verified ORE miners…') via cohort Merkle trees the user joins by proving the relevant state PDA or an attested activity, with only aggregate cohort sizes published. Cut public per-SGT votes, the dApp-rating shaming, flat SKR payouts to respondents, and trustless-receipt claims for apps that have no user PDAs. Grafts: the on-device LiteRT-LM clustering of 'why' answers from Gateman/Airtime; the weekly State of the Seekers report as build-in-public content (the Cashflow precedent); an optional surprise 'snap poll' moment from Whistle/HOTSHOT. Don't graft ORE, because ORE cannot plausibly be primary here. Demo: a real 9pm reveal with hundreds of mainnet SGT voters, a Solscan proof and nullifier on screen with no address linkable to the vote, an automatic rejection of a double vote and of a spoofed SGT, and a sponsor question paid in SKR with BTS payouts landing. Even at its best, this sits in a lane the judges have already seen (TBD, U$ER, opinion markets). Realistically it is a top-10 contender, not a favourite.


## RED TEAM: airtime | verdict=wounded | P(1st)=0.02 | P(top10)=0.12

FEASIBILITY:
- [low] The Payment Channels flow works: a learner session key as payer and authorized_signer, per-second 50-byte cumulative vouchers, and the tutor verifying and settling. -> HOLDS. The program is live on mainnet and was audited by Cantina in July 2026. The voucher is exactly 50 bytes (magic, channel_id, cumulative_amount, expires_at). `open` is signed by the payer, and authorized_signer does not need to sign. `settle` and `distribute` are permissionless, so an operator can settle and pay tutors who hold no SOL. `settleAndSeal` needs the payee's signature. `topUp` can only be called by the payer. rent_payer can be the operator, and it gets its SOL back at close. (github.com/solana-foundation/payment-channels README and docs/003-program-instructions.md (instruction and signer table))
- [medium] The tutor gets paid at hang-up. -> TRAP. `distribute` sends a payout to the treasury, via `PayoutRedirected`, whenever the recipient's canonical ATA is closed, uninitialized, frozen or reassigned. TREASURY_OWNER is the Solana Foundation operator. A new tutor with no USDC ATA would permanently lose earnings to the Foundation unless the app preflights the ATA or creates it idempotently before every distribute. (docs/003-program-instructions.md, distribute section: 'an unusable canonical ATA redirects the amount to treasury… permanently advances past that amount')
- [low] '612 payments. 1 transaction', plus cheap lazy per-pair channels and Tandem mode. -> OVERCLAIM. On-chain this is a single cumulative voucher, i.e. one payment metered off-chain. Each new learner–tutor pair costs open (a 256-byte PDA plus an escrow ATA), then settle/seal, distribute, and reclaim after 1,500 slots: 3 to 4 transactions per pair. Tandem mode doubles that to net out to roughly zero. Channel reuse ('rearm', ADR-005) is an unimplemented v2 proposal. The mechanism works, but the headline invites a 'you metered a $1 call' reaction. (payment-channels README lifecycle and the 'Future work: v2' section)
- [medium] The Subscriptions & Allowances weekly leash limits the damage from a compromised phone. -> HOLDS, BUT THE SECURITY GAIN IS SMALL. That is the same 'unlimited Approve' pattern Solflare-style guards and drainer detectors flag, shown to a security-anxious audience. The session key can pull the whole weekly cap at once, so the worst case equals a plain weekly transfer to the session wallet. (github.com/solana-program/subscriptions README line 7 ('sets it as the single delegate… with u64::MAX approval'), plus the revocation caveats in its Security Considerations)

JUDGE RISK:
- Base rate: none of the roughly 20 grand-prize winners across the two earlier Solana Mobile hackathons was a security or wallet-defence product. Winners were consumer habit, game, social, prediction and yield apps. Security tools have historically scored as infrastructure, not as the consumer habit 'Stickiness & PMF' asks for.
- Toly: 'Mobile is all about consumers' and 'SocialFi is the next breakout'. He is likely to read this as 'a burner wallet with extra steps' and a wallet feature, not an app people want. The Allowances leash sits on a Squads-upgradeable program holding a u64::MAX approval, which conflicts with his no-admin-keys, immutable-software ethos.
- Mert: security is his favourite AI use case, and the product uses Helius heavily, both positives. But a proxy wallet in the signing path is the exact position an attacker wants. A registry that auto-confirms flags through a backend attester is an 'illusion of comfort' admin key. His company is a Seeker Guardian, whose job is flagging malicious apps, so a community registry competes with that role. One missed drainer or one wrongly flagged legitimate dApp ends trust.
- Solana Mobile (Beeman, Akshay, Chase): they are building Seeker Connect to route dApps straight to Seed Vault Wallet, and Guard mode works against that direction. Moving daily activity to a float key takes usage away from the Seed Vault Wallet Activity Tracking they built and pay seasons against. The 'Would you sign this?' puzzle renders a copy of the Seed Vault approval sheet, which is UI spoofing of their system surface and teaches users that Seed Vault-looking sheets can appear inside third-party apps. Solflare Guards and the native 'security warnings' already cover much of it.
- Ethelsec (A2nkF, Voynich): a dual-role MWA endpoint is a man-in-the-middle position. The design deliberately exposes several untrusted-intent entry points (MWA association, PROCESS_TEXT, SEND, QR tile), and the judges' research lists exported activities that handle untrusted intents as a red flag. The Ed25519 day key sits in app memory. The auto-sign-inside-cap design is blind signing. Simulation-based detection can be evaded: TOCTOU and simulation-detecting drainers are documented, and Totem already checks for 'a state change between reading and signing'. A security product that gets an audit finding loses more than any other category.
- Stickiness: the use is insurance-shaped. The daily hooks (puzzle, sweep, digest) are edutainment or shrinking windfalls, and there are no social or network effects beyond flagging. For a farming audience whose score depends on the Seed Vault wallet, the Float is a reason not to use it.
- Legal and governance: SKR-bonded public accusations against named dApps and domains bring defamation exposure, griefing by colluding SGT holders, and a bounty pool that can turn into an 'earn SKR by flagging' farm. The '5% of scam rent' funding is tiny and shrinks as rent falls.
- Prize ceiling: with no ORE role it cannot compete for the ORE match, which is worth up to $30k more at first place and is a real edge for the ORE-core candidates.
- Demo overlap: Totem already shows a red-sheet drainer block with a bundled attacker dApp, so Gateman's strongest demo beat is no longer unique. The only part that stays unique is the 'vault untouched, only $4.80 lost from the float' shot.

MUST FIX:
- Cut Guard mode (the MWA relay to Seed Vault Wallet) from the product and the pitch. Gateman should be an MWA wallet whose accounts are only the leashed day keys, and should call Seed Vault Wallet only as an ordinary dApp for the daily grant, sweep and revoke. That removes the MITM, identity-laundering and Seeker Connect objections at once.
- Never auto-sign. Every float transaction gets a simulated receipt and a one-tap confirm, with a biometric step-up above per-transaction limits. Say this in the README and the demo narration.
- Be honest about the leash. The pre-pulled float is the blast radius, the same as a burner. The Allowances program adds just-in-time pulls and expiry without a sweep. Disclose the u64::MAX approval to the Subscription Authority PDA and the Squads upgrade authority, and tell users which mints (Token-2022 with hooks or permanent delegate, and native SOL) the leash does not cover.
- Handle Seeker Activity scoring directly. Route per dApp: unknown or new dApps go to the float, and allowlisted blue chips (Jupiter and similar) stay on Seed Vault Wallet. Show that the daily grant and sweep are natural, value-bearing vault transactions.
- Check on a real Seeker in week one whether KeyMint Curve25519 is available. If it is not, ship an AES-Keystore-wrapped software key and write the in-memory exposure into THREAT_MODEL.md.
- Make the deterministic rules engine and templates authoritative. The LLM only paraphrases, is sandboxed against injected token names and memos, and is never the thing that approves or blocks. Only claim languages the templates actually cover.
- Restyle the daily puzzle so it clearly does not copy the Seed Vault approval sheet.
- Shield registry: drop Helius auto-confirm, or make the attester key explicit and small with a timelock. Keep optimistic multi-seat confirmation with counter-bonds and dispute by upgrade authority or publisher key. Use neutral wording ('reported by N verified Seekers') to limit defamation risk. No flagging bounties paid in SKR emissions.
- Harden every exported entry point (MWA association, PROCESS_TEXT, SEND, QR). Validate calling package and Digital Asset Links, never log intents or auth tokens, and run the Radiants audit early. A security product cannot ship with a finding.
- Position against Totem, Solflare Guards and the Seed Vault Wallet warnings in the deck, e.g. 'wallet-agnostic, blast-radius-first, public registry', and do not claim the store has zero security apps.
