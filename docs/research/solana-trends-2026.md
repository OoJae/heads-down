########## SOLANA-TRENDS-2026 ##########
SUMMARY: As of late September 2026, Solana leadership is pushing three connected stories: Internet Capital Markets (tokenized stocks and RWAs, fully onchain perps and orderbooks), stablecoin payments, and agentic machine payments (x402, MPP, pay.sh). The Foundation has just shipped the plumbing for all three. Subscriptions & Allowances has been on mainnet since June (audited by Cantina/Spearbit). Payment Channels launched on mainnet on Sept 3 (Cantina audit, 1M payments/sec benchmark, Alibaba Cloud as launch partner). pay-kit ships a Kotlin client, and the Foundation hired a payments GM on Sept 24. The chain is also getting faster in ways a user can feel. Slot time dropped from 400ms to 250ms on Sept 18, transaction v1 raised the size limit to 4KB on Sept 9, and rent is being cut 90% in five steps (SOL can be reclaimed with WithdrawExcessLamports). Alpenglow, which brings finality to about 150ms, went live on testnet Sept 22 and devnet Sept 25. Mainnet is targeted for October but has no firm date, so a demo before the Oct 8 deadline should not depend on it. Stablecoins are the strongest real-usage signal: supply hit a record $17.3B on Sept 25, 9.3M wallets hold USDC, Q3 card spend was a record $262.7M, and Western Union, MoneyGram, Mastercard, SoFi and Cash App all launched on Solana. Tokenized equities are the fastest-growing category: Solana has about 97% of onchain tokenized-equity trading, over 1M holders and $500M in xStocks AUM. The SEC Innovation Exemption (Sept 17) is a tailwind but requires KYC for every wallet and full shareholder rights, which excludes today's synthetic structures. Consumer finance on mobile is already crowded with well-funded apps: FOMO (#3 US iPhone Finance, $550M valuation), Phantom (CASH stablecoin, Visa card, 40x perps, prediction markets), Jupiter Mobile (prediction markets, Earn and Borrow), Solflare (exclusive mobile venue for the Foundation's $1M perps competition) and Rekt on Seeker. Generic trading, perps, copy-trading, prediction-market frontends, gacha packs and memecoin launchpads are all saturated. About 58% of DEX volume is flagged as circular, and Trepa, a prediction app featured by Seeker, shut down in August. Mert (Helius) is focused on privacy: Helius bought Light Protocol and Rings is in devnet beta but not on mainnet yet. Helius Parsed Streams and the Parsed Events API left beta on Sept 25 and cover 3,600+ programs on the free tier, which makes rich real-time mobile feeds cheap to build. DePIN revenue is small (about $2.5M a month across the leaders) apart from Helium. The newer momentum is in crowdsourcing AI and robotics data: CrowdBrain won the Frontier hackathon grand prize, and Kled and BitRobot are growing. The security record for 2026 is poor: Drift lost $286M, the Avici card contract was hacked, and ORE and BonkDAO had incidents. With two security researchers on the panel, visible safe-signing design will count. The most open ground for a winning Seeker app is a daily-habit consumer product built on the new payment primitives (allowances or payment channels), secured by Seed Vault, and using Seeker identity (.skr names, Seeker Genesis Token) and Helius streams. It should present a stablecoin, ICM or agentic story, not another trading terminal.

IMPLICATIONS_FOR_WINNING:
- Build on primitives that are brand new and have not yet been used in a mobile consumer app: Payment Channels (Sept 3, audited, mainnet) and Subscriptions & Allowances (June, audited, mainnet). This earns innovation points and speaks directly to what the Foundation and Toly are promoting (payments, ICM, agentic commerce). Neither has a flagship consumer mobile app yet.
- Pair Seed Vault's biometric or double-tap signing with a 'sign once, then it just works' model (an allowance or payment-channel escrow). That turns crypto friction into a mobile-native UX story (one fingerprint, then streaming or recurring value) that a PWA or web port cannot copy.
- Tell a stablecoin-first story (USDC/CASH, 9.3M holders, $17.3B supply) rather than a speculation story. Judges have seen hundreds of trading, perps and prediction frontends, and the super-apps (Phantom, Jupiter, FOMO, Solflare) already ship them with far more polish.
- Aim at each judge: Helius Parsed Streams and the Parsed Events API (GA Sept 25, free tier) for rich real-time feeds, plus privacy-aware design, for Mert. Onchain markets, payments and latency (250ms slots, Alpenglow later) for Toly. Retention loops, Seeker Genesis Token, .skr names and a meaningful non-staking SKR use for Akshay and Beeman. Visible security for EthelSec: simulate before signing, plain-English signing prompts, capped allowances, no durable-nonce patterns, a verifiable build and a stated threat model.
- Daily stickiness counts for 25%. Design a habit loop (daily streaks, a live money ticker, recurring pulls, notifications driven by Helius streams) and show retention evidence from real testers in the demo. Colosseum-style judging increasingly rewards evidence of user demand.
- Use Seeker identity as an anti-sybil layer: gate rewards per Seeker Genesis Token mint (check balance > 0, signed message, record used mints) and show .skr names instead of addresses. Both are cheap to build and clearly Seeker-native.
- For the SKR prize, avoid staking and plain 'pay with SKR' (already done by NOMAD and SP3ND). Stronger options: SKR as a payment-channel or allowance currency, SKR-funded bounties for crowdsourced data, or SKR bonds and deposits that users lose if they break a commitment.
- If you go for the ORE match, ORE has to be core to the product. A mobile-native ORE experience (for example, habit-driven grid deploys, or earning ORE for useful real-world actions) fits ORE's 'proof of useful work' direction, but be careful about gambling optics and ORE's June exploit history.
- Don't make the demo depend on Alpenglow mainnet or Helius Rings mainnet before Oct 8; neither is guaranteed. Build on live mainnet features (tx v1, 250ms slots, payment channels, allowances, rent reclaim) and present Alpenglow and Rings as roadmap upside.
- Avoid US-geofenced features in the core demo (perps, xStocks, the Seed Vault USDC Earn vault, some prediction markets). Several judges are US-based, and the SEC exemption requires KYC for every wallet.

THINGS_TO_AVOID:
- Memecoin launchpads and pump.fun clones. The category is fatigued and wash-heavy (58% of DEX volume flagged circular), and it is competitive (pump.fun, Pons, Stonkfun, LetsBonk).
- Copy-trading and social-trading terminals. FOMO ($550M valuation, #3 on the iPhone Finance chart) and Jupiter Mobile V3 dominate.
- Generic prediction-market frontends. Phantom (World Prediction Markets), Jupiter Mobile and DFlow/Kalshi integrations already exist, and Trepa (featured by Seeker) shut down in August 2026.
- Gamified mobile perps and leverage apps (Rekt on Seeker, Clash of Perps, Perps Rider, Phantom and Solflare perps). They are also geofenced for US judges and ethically fraught.
- Gacha and pack-opening apps (Collector Crypt, Solflare Packs, Rarible Gacha Station, Phygitals, Beezie, Slabzapp). The category is saturated.
- Blinks/Actions-centric products. The category has been stagnant in 2026 with no new rendering surfaces.
- SKR staking integrations (explicitly disqualified from the SKR prize) and plain 'pay with SKR' checkout (already done by NOMAD and SP3ND).
- PWA or webshell wrappers. Solana Mobile's CLI offers a webshell, but judges explicitly penalize web ports, and Seeker Connect is currently web-only, so a native React Native or Kotlin MWA integration is needed.
- Depending on unreleased infrastructure for the core demo: Alpenglow mainnet (no firm date), Helius Rings mainnet (devnet beta), and Seeker Connect for React Native or Kotlin (only 'planned').
- Deprecated or dead dependencies: Switchboard (shut down Sept 25), Solana ID (shut down), Light Token SDK (being sunset), Bubblegum v1, and hard-coding .sol naming (moving to .sns).
- Token-emission DePIN without real demand. Hivemapper revenue fell 81% and NATIX is cooling; sustainable DePIN needs a buyer for the data or service.
- 'AI agent trading bot' clones and raw x402 volume claims. Adjusted data shows real x402 demand is tiny and concentrated, so judges will see through inflated metrics.
- Weak security hygiene. Many 2026 exploits (Drift durable nonces, the Avici card contract, ORE's missing account check) will be front of mind for the EthelSec judges, so avoid blind signing, unlimited delegations and admin keys without timelocks.
