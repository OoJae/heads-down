Heads Down: your phone's night shift

Put your phone face-down and it mines ORE from your own ORE Automation account. Pick it up and the rig goes cold. Nothing but your phone's own hardware key can switch it on: not a server, not us.

If a wallet shows this address next to a signing prompt, the request comes from the Heads Down Android app. The app asks for one approval when you clock in. It never asks for your seed phrase, and nothing here will ever ask you to connect a wallet on a web page.
