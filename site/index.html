Heads Down: your phone's night shift

Clock in, lay your phone face-down on its charger, and it can mine ORE from your own ORE Automation account. Pick it up and the rig goes cold. The program digs only when a heartbeat signed by a key in your phone's Android Keystore verifies on-chain, and only in rounds when ORE's cost is at or under the ceiling your wallet signed. Our servers cannot forge that heartbeat.

The Heads Down Android app gives your wallet this address as its identity. A wallet cannot verify that yet, and another app could name the same address, so read what the wallet says before you approve. The app opens your wallet to sign in while it makes the rig key, to clock in, to clock out, and to take your SOL back or close the rig. It never asks for your seed phrase, and this page will never ask you to connect a wallet.
